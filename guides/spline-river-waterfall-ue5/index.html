How to build a stylized river and waterfall with splines in UE5

The easiest way to make a river in Unreal Engine 5 is a spline: you draw the riverbed with spline points and a Blueprint lays mesh segments along it with a flowing water material. The same idea pointed downwards gives you a waterfall with foam at the base.

By RafaSC, 3D and technical artist · Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Why a spline

A river is a path, and a path is exactly what a spline describes. Each stretch between two points becomes a piece of mesh that bends to follow the curve. You can reshape the river by dragging points instead of rebuilding geometry, and the water material only has to know one thing: which way is downstream.

Draw the river

With Stylized Water Material the Blueprint is Blueprints/BP_SW_River.

  1. Drag BP_SW_River into the level. Select it and its spline appears.
  2. Move the spline points to draw the riverbed. To add a point, hold Alt and drag an existing one. The river mesh follows the spline.
  3. In the Details panel set the shape.
SettingWhat it does
RiverWidthWidth of the river
SegmentLengthLength of each mesh piece along the spline
RiverMaterialThe water material. The default is MI_SW_05_River
SegmentMeshThe mesh that is repeated along the spline

Keep the flow continuous

The usual problem with segmented rivers is a visible seam where one piece ends and the next begins, because the flow restarts. In this Blueprint the flow does not break between segments. To make the water faster or slower, change FlowSpeed in group 14, River Flow, of the material.

Add the waterfall

  1. Drag Blueprints/BP_SW_Waterfall into the level.
  2. Shape its spline from the lip at the top to the base.
  3. Set Width and EndWidth, so it opens as it falls, and SegmentLength.
  4. ShowSplash adds foam and mist at the impact. ShowRipple adds rings on the water below: turn it off if the base does not land in water.

  • The River preset is one of six. The sea around the river mouth can use Ocean or Tropical; the look is described in how to make stylized water in UE5.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a point to a spline river in Unreal Engine 5?

Select the Blueprint so the spline appears, then hold Alt and drag an existing point to add a new one.

How do I change the speed of the river?

Change FlowSpeed in group 14, River Flow, of the river material.

Why are there rings where my waterfall lands on the ground?

ShowRipple draws rings on the water below. Turn it off if the base of the waterfall does not land in water.

Which Unreal Engine version do these Blueprints need?

They are part of Stylized Water Material, tested on Unreal Engine 5.8.
