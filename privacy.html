RafaSC Publisher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Who uses this app

RafaSC Publisher is a private tool used only by its operator, Rafa Sanchis (RafaSC), to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channel and his own TikTok account. It is not offered to anyone else and no other person can sign in to it.

2. What it accesses

When the account owner authorises the app, the platform issues an access token:

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to, and manage, the owner's own channel. The app uses it only to upload the owner's videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, change their privacy, and read the channel's own public statistics.
  • TikTok: the video.upload permission, used only to send the owner's videos to that same account as drafts.

The app does not access, collect or process data about any other user of either platform.

3. What it stores

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally, on the operator's own computer, and nowhere else. They are never shared, sold, published or sent to any third party. The app has no server, no database and no analytics.

4. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By authorising it you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the data Google receives is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to this app can be reviewed and revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Removing access

YouTube access can be revoked from the Google account permissions page linked above. TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Once revoked, the locally stored token stops working and is deleted.
