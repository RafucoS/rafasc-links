What it is

Stylized Rope & Chain Physics Kit is a C++ rope solver with a full Blueprint API for Unreal Engine 5.8. Drag a Blueprint into the level, move its two ends, press Play. Ropes do not stretch like rubber, do not explode and fall asleep when they stop moving.

Quick start

  1. Enable the plugin: Edit > Plugins > search "Rope Kit" > tick it, restart the editor.
  2. In the Content Browser open Plugins > RopeKit Content > Blueprints (enable Show Plugin Content in the view settings if you do not see it).
  3. Drag BP_RK_Rope (or Chain, Cable, Vine, RopeLadder, HangingLantern, RopeBridge) into your level.
  4. Drag the small diamond (End Point) to where the rope should end. The rope previews live in the viewport.
  5. Press Play.

In short

Stylized Rope & Chain Physics Kit by RafaSC is a C++ rope physics plugin with a full Blueprint API for Unreal Engine 5.8. It includes 7 ready Blueprints (rope, chain, cable, vine, rope ladder, hanging lantern and walkable rope bridge), a winch, cutting and breaking by load, a component for characters to swing, climb and traverse, and multiplayer support for listen and dedicated servers. Its fixed-step solver gives the same result at 30, 60 or 120 fps and had 0 explosions in 378 test runs. Rope-to-rope collision is not included; it was tested on UE 5.8 (Windows, DirectX 12), with UE 5.5-5.7 and mobile not included. It is sold on Fab for $34.99.

What you get

  • 7 ready Blueprints: Rope, Chain (real-size alternating links), Cable, Vine, Rope Ladder, Hanging Lantern and Rope Bridge.
  • Rope From Spline: draw the rope with a spline in the viewport; optionally every spline point becomes a fixed hook (fences, cables over poles).
  • Winch: change the length while it simulates, even with a load hanging from it (SetTargetLength, Reel, OnReelStopped).
  • Cut any rope with one call, or let it break when the load is too heavy. Blueprint events: OnRopeCut (cut or break) and OnRopeBroken (break by load).
  • Real weight: hang a 50 kg crate (Chaos physics body) and the rope carries it; rolling balls push the rope and the rope pushes back.
  • Character interaction component: swing on a vine, climb a rope ladder, hang and traverse a rope, walk across the bridge. Includes an Enhanced Input example character to copy from.
  • Style Data Assets: change mesh, material, thickness and links without touching the physics.
  • Hard stretch limit: after a violent pull (a 25 m/s whip of the whole rope) the stretch stays at 8.2 % or less.
  • Sleep, distance LOD and off-screen skipping: ropes that are not moving (also lying on the floor) cost almost nothing and wake up when something touches them.
  • 7-room showroom map included (swing, bridge, lanterns, chains, ladder and vines, winch and crate, stress test): open it and press Play.
  • Demo map with 9 rooms (ropes, chains, bridge, swing, cables, stress test, comparison and a Torture Test room: 150 kg on one rope, 25 m/s whips, live stretch board) and a playable character with an on-screen ms counter.

Good to know

  • Tested on Unreal Engine 5.8 (Windows, DirectX 12). Not included in this release: UE 5.5-5.7 (a later update is planned) and mobile (not tested).
  • Multiplayer ready (listen and dedicated server): the server decides cuts, breaks, the winch and resets; every machine simulates its own copy and clients are corrected smoothly. Tested with a server and a client at up to 200 ms round trip.
  • The exact rope shape is not sent over the network (the events are), so a loose broken piece may rest a little differently on each machine.
  • Ropes collide with the world and physics bodies; rope-to-rope collision is not included.
  • Beyond 30 m from the camera (LOD Distance) ropes simulate cheaper (fewer sub-steps, collision stays on); adjustable per rope.
  • Everything lives in the plugin folder (/RopeKit). Two modules: RopeKit (the rope system) and RopeKitDemo (demo map, demo character, example character); your game only needs RopeKit.
  • The plugin uses the engine plugins Procedural Mesh Component, Cable Component and Enhanced Input (they come with Unreal and are enabled automatically).

Frequently asked questions

Is it Blueprint only or C++?

The solver is C++ (module RopeKit) and everything is exposed to Blueprint. You do not need to compile anything: the plugin ships ready for UE 5.8 on Windows, and you can build ropes using only Blueprints.

Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes, for listen and dedicated servers. The server decides cuts, breaks, the winch and resets; every machine simulates its own copy and clients are corrected smoothly. Tested with a server and a client at up to 200 ms round trip: same cuts, breaks and winch lengths, 0 explosions. The exact rope shape is not sent, so a loose broken piece may rest a little differently on each machine.

How many ropes can I have, and what do they cost?

Measured on a Ryzen 7 7800X3D + RTX 3080 (game thread): 10 awake ropes with collision 0.33 ms, 50 ropes 1.65 ms, 100 on screen 2.8 ms, 100 off screen 1.2 ms, 100 sleeping ropes 0.02 ms. Your numbers will depend on your hardware.

Does it work with my own character?

Yes. Add the Rope Interact component to your character to swing on a vine, climb a rope ladder, hang and traverse a rope or walk across the bridge. An Enhanced Input example character is included to copy from.

Which Unreal Engine versions are supported?

UE 5.8 on Windows (DirectX 12). UE 5.5-5.7 are not included in this release; a later update is planned.

Does it use Chaos or its own physics?

The rope has its own fixed-step solver (60 Hz with sub-steps), so the result is the same at 30, 60 or 120 fps. It collides with the world and with Chaos physics bodies: a 50 kg crate can hang from a rope and rolling balls push it.

Can I cut or break a rope?

Yes. Cut any rope with one call, or let it break when the load is too heavy. Blueprint events: OnRopeCut (cut or break) and OnRopeBroken (break by load).

Do ropes collide with other ropes?

No. Rope-to-rope collision is not included. Ropes collide with the world and with physics bodies.

Does it work on mobile?

Not tested, so I do not claim it. It is tested on Windows, DirectX 12.

What is in the showroom and the demo, and can I delete them?

The 7-room showroom map and the 9-room demo map (with a playable character) are only for presentation. Your game only needs the RopeKit module: delete the Showroom and Demo folders if you do not need them.
