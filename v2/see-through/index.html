What it is

See your character through walls, roofs, trees and props - with ONE Material Function.

No actor tags. No traces. No C++. No runtime material swaps.

The material knows where the camera is looking, so with top-down and isometric-style perspective cameras it opens a clean hole between the camera and your character by itself - no Blueprint required. Orthographic camera? Untick Auto Calculate Ortho Planes or add the included component. Zooming camera, third person or a party of 4? Drop the included component on your characters. Floors are never cut, and cut walls keep casting their shadows.

Quick start

  1. Open your wall, roof or tree material and set Blend Mode to Masked.
  2. Right-click in the graph, type See-Through and add MF_SeeThrough_Apply.
  3. Connect Opacity Mask to Opacity Mask and Emissive to Emissive Color.
  4. Create a Material Instance and set Focus Distance to your Spring Arm length (1400 in the Top Down Blueprint template).

In short

See-Through Occlusion by RafaSC is a Material Function for Unreal Engine 5 that opens a hole in walls, roofs and trees between the camera and the character, so the player stays visible in top-down, isometric and third-person games. Floors are never cut and cut walls keep casting shadows. It needs Masked materials for the cut-out; a Silhouette post process is included for meshes that cannot be Masked. Tested on UE 5.5 and 5.8; not for the Path Tracer, mobile not tested. It is sold on Fab for $12.99.

What you get

  • Works out of the box with the UE Top Down Blueprint template (Focus Distance 1400 is the default)
  • Drop MF_SeeThrough_Apply into your material and connect 2 pins - or use the ready master / presets
  • Ground Lock: floors, rugs and low props are never cut - also on stairs and upper floors
  • Shadows stay (switchable): the shadow pass keeps your original mask
  • Per-pixel: no popping, no darker overlaps, no actor lists
  • 7 presets: Hole, Dither, Glow, Burn (animated), Ghost, Cutaway, Off
  • Optional BP_SeeThroughTarget component: exact position, zoom-proof, third person, party of up to 4
  • Global controls in one MPC: on/off, zoom override, radius scale
  • Silhouette post process: see the character through ANY material (Opaque, glass, bought assets)
  • Translucent output for glass (smooth fade, no dither)
  • Zero-cost Off preset + Nanite Pixel Programmable Distance support for big levels

Requirements

  • The cut-out needs Masked materials. Can't use Masked? Use the included Silhouette post process instead.
  • Masked costs more than Opaque, especially with Nanite: use it on meshes that block the view, not the whole level.
  • Click-to-move games: set roof/wall collision to ignore the Visibility channel so clicks go through the hole.
  • Dithered styles need TSR or TAA (UE5 default). The clean Hole style works without.
  • Not for the Path Tracer. Mobile not tested.
  • Tested on Windows, DX12 SM6, Nanite + Lumen + Virtual Shadow Maps: UE 5.5 and UE 5.8 (also with Substrate enabled).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Blueprints or to tag my actors?

No tags ever. No Blueprint for a fixed top-down / isometric-style perspective camera. For zoom, third person, orthographic cameras or several characters, add the included component (no programming).

Does it work with my existing materials?

Yes. Add one node and set Blend Mode to Masked. Material Attributes: MF_SeeThrough_Layer. Can't change them? Silhouette mode.

My camera zooms.

One node: set "Focus Distance Override" in MPC_SeeThrough, or add BP_SeeThroughTarget to the character.

My RPG has 4 characters.

Add BP_SeeThroughTarget to each one and pick Target, Target 2, 3 or 4.

Nanite, Lumen and foliage?

Yes, including instanced foliage. Use Pixel Programmable Distance on Nanite meshes to keep far meshes on the cheap Opaque path.

How expensive is it?

One small function in the pixel shader of the materials that use it (411 PS instructions for the full master). The real cost is Masked vs Opaque, so use it on the meshes that can block the view; the Off preset compiles the effect out. No tick, no traces, no material swaps.
